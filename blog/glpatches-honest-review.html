GLpatches are over-the-counter adhesive skin patches sold as "GLP-1" or berberine patches and marketed as weight-loss aids. Despite the name, they do not contain actual GLP-1 medications such as semaglutide or tirzepatide — they are non-prescription topical stickers positioned as a convenient alternative for people curious about GLP-1-style weight support. Typical formulas lean on plant-derived ingredients like berberine, green tea extract and chromium that are commonly associated with metabolic and appetite-support marketing. They are applied to the skin, are not swallowed, and are not a substitute for prescribed medication or professional medical advice.
